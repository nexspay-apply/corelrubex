Who can apply

Built for cardholders.

This enquiry is only for people who already hold an active credit card in their own name. The form checks this first and stays closed if you don't qualify.

✓You can apply if

  • You hold an active credit card from any Indian bank
  • The card is issued in your own name
  • You are the primary cardholder
  • You are 21 years or older

✕You can't apply if

  • You don't hold any credit card yet
  • You only have an add-on (supplementary) card
  • The card belongs to a family member or someone else
  • Your card is closed, blocked or cancelled

FAQs

Good to know.

Still unsure? Start the enquiry and an advisor will answer the rest.

Why must I already hold a credit card?

This enquiry is for existing cardholders looking to upgrade or add a card. If you answer “No” at the first step, the form stays closed and nothing is submitted.

Why is an add-on cardholder not eligible?

Only the primary holder is responsible for the account and can be assessed by the bank on their own credit history.

Does this guarantee approval?

No. ICICI Bank checks eligibility, credit history and documents under its own policies.

Will you ask for my card number or CVV?

Never. We ask only for the last 4 digits. Do not share your full card number, CVV, PIN, OTP or passwords with anyone.

Where do I complete the application?

Only on ICICI Bank's verified official website, app or authorised process.

Ready to find your next card?

Takes about 3 minutes. Open to primary credit cardholders only.
